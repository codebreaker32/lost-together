          Pipeline

          1. Per-hiker camera

            640 × 640 TartanGround forest frames; the predator or a natural decoy is composited at its true distance, and fog is added with the scattering model.

          2. OpenCV 5 detection

            YOLOX-s on the new cv2.dnn engine (ENGINE_NEW). Full-frame detection range: about 15 m.

          3. Look closer

            Uncertain reports are re-checked on a 3× zoom tile, which confirms or dismisses them out to about 20–30 m.

          4. Shared living map

            In sharing conditions, all sightings go to one map and evidence decays over time. A teammate's report triggers a zoom scan.

          5. Decision and human approval

            A rule-based planner and an LLM agent (qwen3-vl on an AWS GPU, calling OpenCV 5 tools through an MCP server) decide when to escape. Escape orders are evacuation broadcasts; a ranger approves or overrides each one.
